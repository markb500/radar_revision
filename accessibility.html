Accessibility statement for the Radar Revision app

This accessibility statement applies to the Radar Revision web application used to support revision for the radar element of the RAF Aerospace Engineering Technician course.

How accessible this website is

Enforcement procedure

The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) enforces the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018. If you are not happy with how we respond to your complaint, contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).

Non-accessible content

  • Mathematical notation via MathJax may be announced unclearly by some assistive technologies (WCAG 1.1.1, 1.3.1).
  • Diagram descriptions are generic where figures are present; complex radar schematics may need more detail (WCAG 1.1.1).
  • Training notes and formula sheet are PDFs whose accessibility depends on file production (WCAG 1.1.1, 2.4.5).

What we’re doing to improve accessibility

  • Visible keyboard focus and a skip link
  • Live regions for questions and solutions
  • Text alternatives when canvas diagrams are used
  • Keyboard reordering in the Test Designer
  • A dedicated control for the separate teacher solution window
